I'm a software engineer and data scientist who builds machine learning models and turns them into real, usable applications. My applied work spans credit scoring, fraud detection, sentiment analysis, and cardiovascular risk prediction aligned with NHS clinical guidelines.

Alongside ML, I build full-stack web applications and dashboards with React JS, Streamlit and Tableau, including M-PESA payment integration for clients who need mobile-money checkout built directly into their product. I bring a human-computer interaction and design-thinking background to that work, so the systems I build are usable and aligned with how real users actually behave, not just technically correct.

I also work as a freelance technical researcher and writer, producing case-study-based academic papers in computer science, a practice that keeps my research and technical-communication skills sharp alongside the engineering work. Two of my repositories have been preserved in GitHub's Arctic Code Vault Archive Program, and I've independently shipped more than 20 deployed applications across healthcare AI, agriculture, computer vision and full-stack web development.
